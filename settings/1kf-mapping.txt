These are the default 1kf controls:
>:1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9 0
>:q w e r t y u i o p
>:a s d f g h j k l semicolon
>:z x c v b n m comma period slash
If you want your 1kf controls to be different, you can find the name of the keys on https://c2.tail.ws/1kf_keys.txt
Note that if the key you use to show the chat and stats screen is also in your 1kf bindings, you will want to change that key
to something else on the in-game keybindings screen to avoid the chat poping up when you press that key during gameplay.

180 modifier key:
>:space
After pressing the 180 modifier, all rows behave as if they were the 180 row for your next piece placement.
Using this feature is optional but it can reduce strain and improve comfort as it reduces vertical finger movement and allows you to play using only three rows.
This works best with a thumb key if you have one.

Topdrop modifier key:
>:rshift
1kf drops the piece in the lowest reachable position by default. 
If you want to drop a piece on the top of your stack when there is a reachable hole under it, 
press the topdrop key and your piece will drop on the top of the stack instead.

SOFTDROP_BY_DEFAULT=1
If you do not want the piece to drop in the lowest position by default, set SOFTDROP_BY_DEFAULT to 0.
Doing so will inverse the behavior such that the piece is dropped in the highest position by default and 
the topdrop modifier needs to be pressed every time you want to drop the piece in the lowest position instead.
This setting is enabled (1) by default because it results in the fewest keys per piece in most situations.
